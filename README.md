# CHX Precision Metal — CNC Machining Manufacturer in Shenzhen, China

**CHX Precision Metal (常合鑫精密五金)** is a CNC machining manufacturer based in Shajing, Bao'an District, Shenzhen, China. Founded in 2013, the company runs a 5,000 m² facility with 100+ employees and 220 machines, producing precision metal and plastic parts for customers in Europe, North America, Japan and Korea.

Website: [chxmetal.com](https://chxmetal.com/en/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile) (English) · [chx-metal.com](https://chx-metal.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile) (中文)

## Capabilities

| Process | Equipment | Range |
|---|---|---|
| 5-axis CNC milling | 100 machines (30 configured 3+2) | up to 1,200 mm envelope |
| CNC turning | 50 lathes | Ø1–300 mm |
| Swiss-type turning | 70 Tsugami sliding-head lathes | Ø1–20 mm (B206: Ø2.0–20 mm) |
| Surface finishing | outsourced, qualified vendors | anodizing, passivation, plating, bead blasting |

Monthly turning capacity reaches 5 million pieces. Materials include aluminium 6061-T6 / 7075-T6 / 5083, stainless steel 303 / 304 / 316L, brass, copper and titanium.

## Tolerances and lead times

- Standard tolerance: **±0.005 mm**, or better where the drawing requires it
- Looser, non-critical features follow **ISO 2768-m**
- Prototyping: 3–7 working days
- Mass production: 7–15 working days
- Surface finishing: 3–7 working days
- **MOQ: 1 piece** — small batches and samples accepted

## Quality and certifications

- ISO 9001:2015 — certificate NOA198428
